SMASHKIN LEGAL WEBSITE — BEFORE PUBLISHING

1. Replace [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME] and [CONTACT EMAIL] in both HTML documents.
2. Verify actual SDKs, analytics, crash reporting, age rating, child-directed status, data storage, consent implementation, and Google Play Data safety / Apple privacy labels. Adjust the policy to accurately reflect the game.
3. Create a PUBLIC GitHub repository named smashkin-legal and upload index.html, privacy-policy.html, terms.html to its root.
4. In repository Settings > Pages, select Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) and save.
5. After publication, URLs will generally be:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/smashkin-legal/privacy-policy.html
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/smashkin-legal/terms.html
6. Confirm both URLs load publicly over HTTPS without login, downloads or region restrictions before adding them to store consoles.

These are starter templates, not legal advice. Confirm applicable requirements and accuracy before publication.
